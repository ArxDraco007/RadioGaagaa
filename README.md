# RadioGaagaa
All we hear is radio!!!

## Why I made this - 
I love radios! I have listened to radios ever since I was introduced to it, when I was 10. I listen to old Tamil songs, Tamil is the language spoken in Tamil Nadu, in the southern part of India. Listening to beautiful songs from 4-5 decades ago, truly showed me how beautiful of a time that was. Fallout was my first serious HackClub event, and when I went to a show and tell, people were developing their own radio, since then I wanted to make my own, and I am grateful to HackClub for this opportunity.
